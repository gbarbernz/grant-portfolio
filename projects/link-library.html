THE CHALLENGE

Good information only helps when people can find it.

Disability services, support information and community resources can be spread across a large number of external sources.

A curated library brings useful links together in one place. Its structure needs to help people scan a broad range of information and identify a resource that is relevant to them.

The project also considers how a centre can extend its reach into the community by sharing resources through a familiar Facebook post and comment format.

COMMUNITY LINK LIBRARY OVERVIEWRecommended image: 1600 × 1000

THE APPROACH

Curate, organise and make the next step clear.

Information hierarchy and simple navigation help turn a large collection of links into a resource people can use.

Resources are grouped so people can browse disability services, support information, community resources and other useful external information. Clear labels make the purpose of each link easier to understand before opening it.

Facebook posts and comments provide a distribution method that can point people towards the library and extend access beyond the centre.

INFORMATION DESIGN

Help people move from a question to a useful resource.

Clear categories and concise link descriptions make it easier to scan and compare a range of external information.

A simple navigation structure supports accessibility and reduces the effort involved in finding a starting point that fits a person's needs.
