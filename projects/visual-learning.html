THE CHALLENGE

Complex information needs a clear way in.

A learning resource can use visual hierarchy and illustration to help people make sense of information without adding unnecessary complexity.

Different content calls for different formats. An infographic can organise related ideas, a flashcard can focus attention on one item, and a visual instruction can make a sequence easier to follow.

The design challenge is to simplify information while preserving what matters, making each resource clear, accessible and relevant to its learning purpose.

THE APPROACH

Give each idea a visual structure.

Information design begins with deciding what a learner needs to notice, understand or do.

Content is organised into a clear hierarchy, then paired with illustrations and visual cues that support the message. Short sections, consistent labels and purposeful grouping help the learner move through the information.

By combining illustration with learning design, a resource can be engaging while still keeping accessibility and comprehension at the centre.

VISUAL DESIGN

Let the visuals clarify, not compete.

Illustration can make an idea more approachable, while careful information design gives each element a clear purpose and place.

Consistent visual hierarchy helps learners distinguish key points, follow instructions and connect supporting images with the information they explain.

VISUAL LEARNING PRINCIPLES

01

Be clear

Keep the main learning point easy to identify.

02

Structure information

Use hierarchy and grouping to support understanding.

03

Design accessibly

Make the visual information readable and approachable.

04

Illustrate with purpose

Connect each image to the idea it helps explain.
