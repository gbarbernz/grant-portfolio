THE CHALLENGE

Feedback can teach while keeping learners engaged.

Scenario-based learning gives learners a chance to make choices. The way feedback responds can make that interaction feel more human and memorable.

Harry is an illustrated English character designed to appear within digital learning scenarios. Consistent character design gives learners a recognisable presence to encounter as they work through an interaction.

Humour, voice and expressive reactions create a platform for wrong-answer feedback that can respond to learner choices without losing sight of the learning point.

HARRY IN A LEARNING SCENARIORecommended image: 1600 × 1000

THE APPROACH

Let expression carry the feedback.

Facial expression and body language give Harry a way to react clearly to what the learner does.

Reactions can escalate when a learner repeats a wrong answer, introducing humour while signalling that it may be time to reconsider. A defined voice helps keep the character's responses consistent across interactions.

The illustrations also have animation potential, creating room for movement and timing to support interactive learning rather than distract from it.

CHARACTER DESIGN

Personality lives in the details.

A consistent base design makes Harry recognisable, while changes in expression and posture communicate his response in each moment.

That visual range can support a distinct character voice, create opportunities for animation and make feedback feel connected to the scenario.
