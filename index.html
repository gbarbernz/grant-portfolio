HOW I WORK

Start with the person, not the software.

Good learning design starts by understanding the person, the environment and what needs to change.

Technology comes afterwards. The goal is not simply to produce content — it is to create something people can understand and use.

ABOUT

Design experience.
Disability experience.
Brought together.

My background combines professional design with substantial frontline disability-sector experience.

I have worked across disability support, community engagement, programme facilitation and creative practice alongside a background in design communication.

I am now bringing those disciplines together through learning design, capability development, inclusive communication and digital learning.
